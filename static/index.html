      Your data

      Frontline has no account or cloud. To move to a new computer, back up here and restore the file there: topics, researchers, papers, summaries, stars, library, ideas, projects, tasks, deadlines and conferences come back exactly as they were. API keys in .env aren't included: copy that file yourself.

      Follow a researcher

      Every paper they co-author shows up here, so you see everything coming out of their lab. Pick the right person: many names are shared. Can't find them by name? Paste their Google Scholar profile link instead: their Scholar papers are used as-is, and new papers found elsewhere are only kept if they share co-authors with them.
